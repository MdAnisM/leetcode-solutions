## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used an array-based approach to find two numbers whose sum is equal to the target. The program checks the elements and compares their sum with the target value. When the required pair is found, their indices are returned.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

I learned that the input can contain duplicate values and the same element cannot be used twice. I also tested the solution with normal and edge-case inputs before submitting it to LeetCode.
